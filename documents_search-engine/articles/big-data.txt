The exponential growth of digital information has given rise to the era of Big Data, an ecosystem characterized by the sheer volume, velocity, variety, and veracity of information generated across the globe. Big Data encompasses both structured data neatly organized in relational databases and massive troves of unstructured data, such as multimedia files, social media streams, and sensor logs from the Internet of Things (IoT). Traditional data processing software is entirely inadequate for managing this scale of information, necessitating distributed storage frameworks and parallel processing architectures. In the context of AI and Machine Learning, Big Data acts as the essential fuel; the predictive accuracy of neural networks and the linguistic fluency of NLP models are directly proportional to the massive datasets upon which they are trained. Consequently, effective data engineering, preprocessing, and data cleaning pipelines are critical prerequisites for transforming raw, heterogeneous Big Data into a refined state suitable for advanced computation.
