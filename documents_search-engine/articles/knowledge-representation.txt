While Big Data provides the raw material, Data Mining provides the specialized techniques required to extract valuable, hidden knowledge and actionable patterns from these colossal datasets. Often overlapping with machine learning, data mining specifically focuses on the exploratory phase of data science—answering "what happened" and "why it happened" through descriptive and explanatory analysis of historical records. Techniques such as association rule mining (frequently used in market basket analysis), anomaly detection (crucial for fraud prevention and network security), and complex clustering are deployed to reveal unexpected segmentations within the data. Although data mining heavily utilizes statistical methods and machine learning algorithms to achieve its goals, its primary objective is human-centric knowledge discovery rather than solely building automated predictive models. Together, the synergistic integration of Data Mining, Big Data, NLP, Knowledge Representation, and Machine Learning forms the comprehensive bedrock of modern intelligent analytics systems.
