Natural Language Processing (NLP) serves as the critical intersection between Artificial Intelligence, computational linguistics, and machine learning, enabling computers to comprehend, interpret, and generate human language in a meaningful way. Historically reliant on structural syntax and semantic rules derived from Knowledge Representation, modern NLP has been revolutionized by deep learning architectures and large language models (LLMs). Today, NLP algorithms effortlessly handle a vast array of linguistic tasks, including sentiment analysis, machine translation, named entity recognition, speech-to-text transcription, and automated text summarization. By mapping words and phrases into high-dimensional vector spaces and leveraging attention mechanisms, NLP models can parse the nuanced context, tone, and underlying intent of human communication. This capability not only powers conversational chatbots and virtual assistants but also allows enterprises to extract actionable insights from unstructured textual data.
